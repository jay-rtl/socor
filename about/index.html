01 / A FOUNDATION FOR THE FUTURE
1983INCORPORATED JULY 13

Founded by collaboration.
Built on commitment.

SOCOR Construction Corporation was incorporated on July 13, 1983. Specializing in horizontal civil works, the company has branches in Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao and is capable of engaging in projects anywhere in the Philippines.

SOCOR has worked with international construction companies including TOA, Taisei, Kajima, Toyo, Hanjin, Interbeton/Ham Dredging, John Holland, Anderson and China Water.

01 / MISSION

Deliver value.
Build relationships.

To provide construction services with high quality, efficiency and competitive pricing to our customers.

To build relationships with our customers, suppliers and subcontractors in achieving goals generating value and mutual benefits throughout the business process.

02 / VISION

A shared ambition.

To be among the leading horizontal construction companies in the Philippines.

03 / QUALITY POLICY

Standards that shape our work.

Socor is an organization committed to consistently providing products & services that satisfy the applicable requirements of our interested parties. We are committed to implementing quality management systems & continuous improvement in the organization as part of our efforts to achieve customer satisfaction.
